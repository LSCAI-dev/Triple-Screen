How Triple Screen works (plain English)

Elder's rule: trade with the tide, buy against the wave, enter only when price confirms. Each screen is a filter; a stock must pass all three, in order.

ScreenQuestionWhat to look for
1 · Tide
weekly chart		Which way is the main trend?Weekly MACD-histogram rising and the 26-week EMA rising. Up-tide = only look for buys. Weekly Impulse red (13-week EMA and histogram both falling) = no buying.
2 · Wave
daily chart		Has price pulled back to fair value?In an up-tide, wait for a dip into the value zone (shaded band between the daily 13- and 26-EMA). Signs the dip is ending: RSI back to 40–50, Bear Power below zero and turning up, Force Index(2) below zero.
3 · Entry
next session		Is the dip over?Place a buy-stop 1 tick above today's high. It only fills if buyers push price up through it. Stop below the recent low; take profit at resistance.

Pullback vs Rising vs Extended (the Wave column). In an up-tide the daily price is in one of three states:

PullbackPrice has dipped toward the EMAs, or RSI/Force Index show a dip. This is the buy zone: a setup can form.
RisingTrend up, price moving up, no dip yet. Wait.
ExtendedPrice has already rallied and closed more than 1.0 ATR above the 20-EMA (the dotted orange line on the daily chart). Buying here means chasing: the stop is far away, reward:risk is poor, and price often drifts back to the EMAs first. Wait for it to return to the value zone.

Example: 20-EMA S$5.00, ATR S$0.10. Close above S$5.10 = Extended. A dip back to roughly S$4.95–5.05 (the value zone) is where the next setup would come from.

The site runs this classic method (Status column) and a stricter scored version (Dual column) that adds volume confirmation and hard gates. Both are explained below.

1. Status: original Elder Triple Screen

Elder's idea (Trading for a Living, 1993): trade in the direction of the long timeframe, enter on a short-timeframe move against it, and let the market pull you in with a stop order.

ScreenRule used here
1 · Tide (weekly)Up = weekly MACD-histogram rising and (26-week EMA rising or close above it). Down = histogram falling and (EMA falling or close below it). Otherwise Mixed → No trade. "Strong" = histogram and EMA agree.
2 · Wave (daily)Up-tide needs a pullback: Force Index(2) < 0, or RSI(14) ≤ 45, or the low touched the 20-EMA (within 0.5%). Close must be ≥ 97% of the 50-EMA and not more than 1.0 ATR above the 20-EMA (too extended). Shorts mirror this (RSI ≥ 55).
3 · EntryBuy-stop 1 tick above today's high, valid 3 sessions (trail it down to each new high if unfilled). Stop = lower of 2-day low − 1 tick and entry − 0.75 ATR. T1 = first resistance > 0.25 ATR above entry, else 2R; T2 = next level, at least 2R.

Grade counts 4 checks: touched the 20-EMA value zone · daily MACD-histogram turned up · R:R to T1 ≥ 2 · strong tide. A = 3+ checks and R:R ≥ 1.5; B = 2+ and R:R ≥ 1.2; else C. If R:R to T1 < 1.0 the setup is downgraded to Watch.

When the STI is bearish (below a falling 50-EMA) both BUY thresholds rise by 10. "Blocked by" on each stock lists exactly which gate or score failed.

5. Dual-score trade plan & management

EntryBuy-stop 1 tick above today's high (SGX ticks: 0.001 below S$0.20, 0.005 below S$1, 0.01 from S$1), valid 2 sessions.
StopSafeZone: today's low − 2.5 × the average amount the low was undercut over 10 sessions; never closer than 0.75 ATR to entry.
T1First resistance at least 1 ATR above entry (swing highs over 250 days + 52-week high, clustered), else 2R.
T2Nearer of the 2nd resistance and the top of the price channel (22-EMA envelope holding 95% of the last 100 bars), at least T1 + 0.5 ATR.
SizeRisk 1.0% of equity (S$100,000 in settings) ÷ (entry − stop), in 100-share lots.
ManageMove stop to breakeven at +1R → sell half at T1 → exit the rest on a close below the 20-EMA → time stop after 7 sessions if under +0.5R → max hold 30 sessions.

R = profit or loss in units of the initial risk (entry − stop). +2R = made twice what was risked. MFE / MAE on Saved runs = best and worst move from the fill, in R.

7. Glossary

EMA
Exponential moving average; weights recent prices more.
MACD / histogram
12-EMA minus 26-EMA, with a 9-period signal line; histogram = MACD minus signal. Its slope is Elder's main trend tool.
RSI
Relative Strength Index (14): 0–100 measure of recent gains vs losses.
ATR
Average True Range (14): typical daily range, used to size stops and distances.
Force Index (2)
2-day EMA of (price change × volume). Below 0 in an up-trend = pullback (original system only).
Bear Power
Elder-ray: daily low − 13-EMA. Negative but rising in an up-trend = sellers fading.
Value zone
Area between the fast and slow EMAs (here the daily 13- and 26-EMA, shaded on the chart) where Elder buys pullbacks.
Extended
Price has run too far above value (close > 20-EMA + 1.0 ATR). Not a sell signal: the trend may still be up, but a new buy has poor reward:risk. Wait for the pullback.
Impulse System
Green = 13-EMA and MACD-histogram both rising; red = both falling; blue = mixed.
SafeZone
Elder's stop placed beyond normal noise: recent average downside penetration × a factor.
OBV
On-Balance Volume: running total adding volume on up days, subtracting it on down days.
VWAP
Volume-weighted average price; "anchored" = measured from a chosen start (here the 60-day low).
CLV
Close location value: 0 = closed at the low, 1 = at the high.
Tick rule
A trade (or hourly bar) above the previous price counts as buying, below as selling.
Fibonacci retracement
How much of the last up-swing a pullback has given back; 38.2–61.8% is the classic buy zone.
R:R
Reward to risk: (target − entry) ÷ (entry − stop).
