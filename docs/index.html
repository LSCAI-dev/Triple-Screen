How the three screens decide

Screen 1, weekly tide. The slope of the weekly MACD histogram (12/26/9) sets direction, confirmed by the 26-week EMA rising or price above it. Up allows only longs; down allows only shorts; mixed means stand aside.

Screen 2, daily wave. Trade against the wave, with the tide. In an up-tide a long qualifies when the 2-day Force Index is below zero, RSI(14) is at or under 45, or the day's low touched the 20-day EMA, with price no more than 3% under the 50-day EMA. Shorts mirror this.

Screen 3, entry. Buy stop one SGX tick above the last day's high. Stop one tick below the lower of the last two lows, but never closer than 0.75 ATR. T1 is the nearest resistance cluster above entry; T2 is the next level or 2R, whichever is higher.

Grade. One point each for touching the value zone (EMA20), daily MACD histogram turning in the trade direction, R:R to T1 of at least 2, and weekly MACD-H and EMA agreeing. A = 3+ points with R:R ≥ 1.5; B = 2 points with R:R ≥ 1.2; C = the rest.

Support and resistance. Swing highs and lows (5 bars each side) over the last 250 sessions, merged when within 0.6 ATR. The count in brackets is how many swings formed the level.
